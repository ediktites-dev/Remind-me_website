1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the REMINDME+ mobile application, associated websites, subscription/payment interfaces, customer support channels, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). By registering for or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy and the applicable consent notices.

2. Personal data we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, mobile number, email address, login/authentication information and account identifiers.
  • Reminder information: voice notes, text, titles, dates, times, recurring schedules, checklists, notes, categories and other information you choose to store in the App.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, App version, IP address, diagnostic information, crash logs, security events and similar technical data.
  • Notification information: notification preferences, reminder delivery status and related interaction information.
  • Location information: location data only when required for an enabled feature, including the SOS/emergency feature, and subject to applicable device permissions and consent.
  • Emergency/SOS information: the emergency contact/family member name, phone number and other information you voluntarily provide for emergency assistance.
  • Subscription/payment information: transaction identifiers, subscription status and payment-related information. Full card/UPI credentials may be processed by authorised payment service providers and are not necessarily stored by the Company.
  • Support and communications: information you provide when contacting support, reporting an issue, or communicating with us.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and maintain your account and provide the reminder/companion functionality.
  • To create, schedule, deliver and manage reminders and notifications requested by you.
  • To process voice notes and convert them into reminder-related information where that functionality is enabled.
  • To provide, activate and operate the SOS/emergency feature when you choose to use it.
  • To communicate with you about your account, subscriptions, service notices, security events and support requests.
  • To process subscriptions and payments through authorised payment providers.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, malware, security incidents and misuse.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the Services, including reliability and performance.
  • To comply with applicable law, legal process, regulatory requirements and lawful requests.

4. Voice notes and AI/processing features

REMINDME+ may process voice notes or other content you submit in order to understand the reminder you intend to create and provide the requested functionality. Where third-party infrastructure, cloud hosting, speech-to-text, AI or other technology providers are used, data may be processed by those providers strictly as required to provide the relevant service and subject to applicable contractual and security controls. The exact vendors and processing arrangements may change as the Services evolve; material changes will be reflected in this Policy where required.

5. SOS / emergency location feature

The SOS feature is an optional emergency-support feature. If you activate SOS and have granted the required permissions, REMINDME+ may use your device location to generate and/or share your current or recent location with the emergency contact(s) you have designated in the App.

  • You are responsible for entering accurate and current emergency-contact information and for ensuring that the designated person is willing to receive such information.
  • Location sharing depends on device permissions, GPS/network availability, internet/mobile connectivity, device settings and third-party services. We do not guarantee that a location will always be available, accurate, current or successfully delivered.
  • REMINDME+ is not an emergency response service, police service, ambulance service, hospital, security service or substitute for contacting local emergency authorities.
  • In a life-threatening emergency, users should contact the appropriate local emergency service directly.
  • You should not rely exclusively on the App for personal safety or emergency response.

6. Location data and permissions

Location access is requested only for features that require it, such as SOS. You may control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location permission may prevent or limit the operation of location-dependent features.

7. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data merely because you use the App. We may disclose or provide access to data where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to:

  • Cloud hosting, storage, analytics, notification, security, AI/speech-processing, customer-support and other technology service providers.
  • Payment gateways and financial/transaction service providers for subscription and payment processing.
  • Emergency contacts selected by you, when you activate the SOS feature and the relevant information is required to provide that feature.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, or service providers where reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Government authorities, courts, regulators, law-enforcement agencies or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

8. Data security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse or destruction. Depending on the relevant system and data, safeguards may include encryption in transit and/or at rest, access controls, authentication, logging, monitoring, backups, secure infrastructure, vulnerability management and other security measures.

No digital service, internet transmission, device, server or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, while we take security seriously and continuously work to strengthen the security architecture of REMINDME+, we cannot promise that the Services will be immune from every possible cyberattack, technical failure, unauthorised access, malware event, data loss or security breach.

The Company will make reasonable efforts to maintain policies, processes and technical safeguards consistent with applicable requirements and will update its practices when laws, regulations, governmental directions or regulatory requirements applicable to the Services change. Nothing in this Policy is intended to exclude or limit any obligation that cannot legally be excluded or limited under applicable law.

9. Compliance with Indian laws and regulations

EDIKT MEDIA WORLDWIDE PRIVATE LIMITED intends to operate REMINDME+ in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of India. This includes, as applicable to the Services and the Company, requirements relating to privacy and personal-data protection, information technology, cybersecurity, intellectual property rights, consumer protection, electronic transactions, taxation, payment systems and other applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

10. Third-party servers, cloud services and OTP providers

The Company will endeavour to select reputable and security-conscious service providers; however, third-party infrastructure means that certain processing may occur through systems outside the Company’s direct physical control. The Company’s responsibility in relation to such providers remains subject to applicable law, contractual arrangements and the limitations stated in this Policy.

OTP services may be used for registration, authentication, login verification, account recovery or other security-related purposes. Users should never share an OTP with another person. The Company will not intentionally request that a user disclose an OTP through an unauthorised channel.

In addition to security controls maintained by our third-party providers, REMINDME+ will implement additional security measures at the application and organisational level to provide an additional layer of protection for user information. These may include encryption, access controls, authentication controls, monitoring, logging, secure API practices, vulnerability management, incident-response procedures and other safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data and Services.

The Company will take reasonable steps to evaluate the security practices and reliability of relevant third-party service providers and, where appropriate, will require appropriate confidentiality, security and data-protection safeguards. However, no third-party system or internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely immune from cyberattacks, outages, unauthorised access or other security incidents.

REMINDME+ may use carefully selected and verified third-party technology providers, including cloud hosting/storage providers, infrastructure providers, OTP/SMS authentication providers, notification providers, payment service providers, AI/speech-processing providers and other technical service providers required to operate the Services. Such providers may process or store information on behalf of the Company for the purposes for which their services are engaged and subject to applicable contractual, technical and security requirements.
